# Where the sounds come from

Every clip here is cut from a recording on [Freesound](https://freesound.org) by
`pipeline/build_audio.py`, which lists the exact stretch each one is taken from. They
come from Freesound's public previews of the recordings (192 kbps Ogg Vorbis).
